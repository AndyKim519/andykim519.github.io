Music Transcriber

In development

Turns audio recordings into sheet music — PDF plus MusicXML (MuseScore / Finale / Sibelius / Dorico) and MIDI — for a chosen target instrument, then renders the score back to audio so you can hear it. The bigger goal: convert any music into a playable arrangement for the instrument or ensemble of your choice.
